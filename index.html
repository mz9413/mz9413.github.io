Mingjia Zhang

I am an assistant professor in the math department at Princeton University. I am interested in arithmetic geometry, in particular, p-adic Hodge theory and the Langlands program. I completed my PhD at Bonn University in 2023, under the supervision of Prof. Dr. Peter Scholze.
